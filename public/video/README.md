# Homepage montage v3

39-second silent H.264 montage, 1280×720 / 24 fps, approximately 8 MB.
Exact source intervals, output durations and production contribution labels
are in src/data/hero-montage.json, also used by the visible synchronized credits.
The timeline is aligned to encoded frames. Credits identify the artist's
contribution to each production, without implying sole authorship of the shot.

The selection follows Juan's professional gallery and supplied references:
- Lady Everest: suspension bridge, rocky valley, climbing terrain and glacier.
- Luck: Land of Luck city, dragon office, scooter structure and purple room.
- SuperThings: workshop machine, robotic dinosaur and surrounding city.
- Angry Birds: island vegetation, cave set dressing and egg prop.
- Paris & Pups: garden and furnished interior; Audi: workshop props.
- Sky Rojo: the facade and wide shot of Juan's modeled building in the explosion.
- Sin huellas: glass impact with the side mirror visible, from Juan's exact clip.

Short source shots are slowed to give sets and props more screen time. Cuts
exclude adjacent dialogue, title cards and unrelated close-ups. The two VFX
productions appear together; a Lady Everest glacier closes the loop.
Sky Rojo is credited to building modeling and surfacing, as identified in the
supplied clip; the VFX excerpts do not imply effects simulation work. The two
Shows cards now open these contribution clips, retaining their trailer URLs
in the source data. No gallery content is removed.

Other Shows and galleries were considered. Morgan's linked trailer was
unavailable. Exploradores and Mary/Joseph have no trailer in the site and no
matching verified source was found. Thelma and Aku remain in their asset galleries.

Whole source frames and source marks are retained; no audio; faststart enabled.
Cover framing adapts the hero to the viewport. Reduced-motion and data-saving
preferences keep the image carousel without requesting the MP4. Playback
failure uses the carousel; video pauses offscreen. Demo Reel stays separate.
Versioned filenames prevent cached video from being paired with new timing
data. Previous montages remain available for cached pages.
