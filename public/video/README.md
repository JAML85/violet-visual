# Homepage montage v2

30-second silent H.264 montage, 1280×720 / 24 fps, approximately 7 MB.
Exact source intervals and production-level contribution labels are in
src/data/hero-montage.json, also used by the visible synchronized credits.
Credits do not imply sole authorship of every visible element.

Selection: all Shows entries and professional galleries were considered.
Lady Everest and Angry Birds receive the longest passages for landscapes,
foliage, set dressing and props matching the gallery. Paris adds colorful sets;
Sky Rojo is a brief live-action production excerpt. Sin huellas was reviewed
but omitted because the trailer is dominated by dialogue and faces.
Morgan's linked trailer was unavailable; its stills were reviewed.
Exploradores and Mary/Joseph have no trailer in the site and no matching
verified source was found. Thelma and Aku remain in their asset galleries.
No gallery content is removed.

Whole source frames retained in the encoded asset; no audio; faststart enabled.
Cover framing adapts the hero to the viewport. Source marks retained in the file.
Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences keep the image carousel without
requesting the MP4. Playback failure uses the carousel; video pauses offscreen.
Demo Reel remains separate. Versioned filenames prevent stale cached video
from being paired with new timing data. Previous montage retained for cached pages.
